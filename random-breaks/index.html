Random team breaks

Lower-priced spots, teams assigned live on stream. Here's how random team breaks work at Outta Nowhere Cards.

How it works

  1. Grab a spot. Random spots cost less than picking a specific team.
  2. Teams are assigned live. Once the spots are filled, each spot gets its team from Whatnot's random wheel, on stream for everyone to see.
  3. We rip the product. Every card for your assigned team is yours: base, rookies, parallels and hits.

Why go random?

  • Cheaper way into high-end product
  • Every spot has a shot at the hit of the break
  • Sleepers happen: smaller teams can land the big card

Special spots

Some products include cards that don't belong to a team. When a product has celebrity or non-sports cards, like 2026 Topps Allen & Ginter, the break includes a Non-Sports / Celebs spot, and we sometimes save one spot for the end of the break to keep the energy up all the way through.

Questions

How are teams assigned?

With Whatnot's random wheel, live on stream after the spots are filled, so everyone sees the result at the same time. See the full break rules.

Is a random spot cheaper than a team break?

Usually, yes. Exact spot prices depend on the product and are set during the show on Whatnot.

What sports do you run random breaks for?

Any sport, plus non-sports when a product has them. Follow outta_nowhere_cards on Whatnot to see the next lineup.
