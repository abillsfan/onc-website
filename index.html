2026 Topps Heritage Football · Hobby Box

Heritage Football is back for the first time since 2015, putting today's NFL stars and rookies on Topps' classic 1976 football design. Old favorites like League Leaders and Real One Autographs return, joined by new inserts built around 1976: All-Pro Series, a nod to that year's 24-card Wonder Bread set featuring 2025's All-Pros, and The Expansion, covering all 28 teams from the league's 1976 expansion season.

Hobby Box: 20 packs of 8 cards (160 cards), with 1 autograph or relic per box. Topps release date: Oct 21, 2026

Questions? We got you.

What is a card break?

A break is when sealed boxes or packs are opened live and the cards are split among the buyers. In a team break, you buy a team and get every card for that team that comes out of the product. Full guide to card breaks.

What sports do you carry?

Any card, any sport — baseball, football, basketball and more. Product changes show to show, so follow us on Whatnot and Facebook to see what's on deck.

When are the shows?

Follow outta_nowhere_cards on Whatnot and turn on notifications — you'll get an alert when we go live. Upcoming shows are also posted on our Facebook page.

How do I pay and get my cards?

All purchases happen through Whatnot, so checkout and payment are handled securely in the app. After the show, your cards are sent to the address on your Whatnot account.

Do I need to be watching to win?

For auctions and breaks you'll want to be in the live show to bid. Once you've bought a spot, your cards are yours whether you stay to watch or not — but it's way more fun live.

What are the break rules?

Random teams are assigned with Whatnot's random wheel, all cards ship (base included), non-team card assignments are announced in the show, and breaks run during scheduled shows. Full break rules & FAQ.

How do I contact ONC?

Email us at info@on-cards.com with questions about a break or an order. You can also message us on Facebook or Whatnot.
