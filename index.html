Break formats

Not sure what you're buying into? Here's how each format works on an ONC show.

Most popular

Team Breaks

Buy a team, get every card for that team pulled from the box. Teams are sold one at a time — you get your team, not a pairing — unless a combo is specifically requested.

Random Breaks

Grab a spot at a lower price and get randomly assigned a team live on stream. Could be a sleeper, could be the hit of the night.

Treasure Box Pull

A tiered pull with level-up chances. Pull your card, and if you land the right tier, you level up to the next box for a shot at something bigger.

Sudden Death Auctions

Fast-paced auctions where the last bid standing when the timer hits zero takes it. Stay sharp — these move quick.

Buyer Appreciation Giveaways

BAG giveaways are our way of saying thanks to the folks who show up and support the stream. Hang out, have fun, and you might walk away with something extra.

Questions? We got you.

What is a card break?

A break is when sealed boxes or packs are opened live and the cards are split among the buyers. In a team break, you buy a team and get every card for that team that comes out of the product.

What sports do you carry?

Any card, any sport — baseball, football, basketball and more. Product changes show to show, so follow us on Whatnot and Facebook to see what's on deck.

When are the shows?

Follow outta_nowhere_cards on Whatnot and turn on notifications — you'll get an alert when we go live. Upcoming shows are also posted on our Facebook page.

How do I pay and get my cards?

All purchases happen through Whatnot, so checkout and payment are handled securely in the app. After the show, your cards are packed and shipped to the address on your Whatnot account.

Do I need to be watching to win?

For auctions and breaks you'll want to be in the live show to bid. Once you've bought a spot, your cards are yours whether you stay to watch or not — but it's way more fun live.

How do I contact ONC?

Email us at info@on-cards.com with questions about a break, an order or shipping. You can also message us on Facebook or Whatnot.
