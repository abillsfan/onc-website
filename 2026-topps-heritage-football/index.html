2026 Topps Heritage Football breaks

Heritage Football is back for the first time since 2015, putting today's NFL stars and rookies on Topps' classic 1976 design. What's in a 2026 Topps Heritage Football Hobby Box, and what we pulled from it live on Whatnot.

What's in a hobby box

  • Released October 21, 2026
  • Hobby Box: 20 packs of 8 cards (160 cards)
  • 1 autograph or relic per hobby box

About the product

Old favorites like League Leaders and Real One Autographs return, joined by new inserts built around 1976: All-Pro Series, a nod to that year's Wonder Bread set featuring 2025's All-Pros, and The Expansion, covering all 28 teams from the league's 1976 expansion season. Other inserts include Sweetness and New Age Performers, plus Chrome Refractors down to Red /5 and the SuperFractor 1/1.

The rookie class includes Fernando Mendoza, Jeremiyah Love, Carnell Tate, Arvell Reese, Jordyn Tyson, Kenyon Sadiq, Caleb Downs and Cade Klubnik.
