5 new sports card releases dropping Thanksgiving week

Nov 24, 2026 · 1 min read · Outta Nowhere Cards

Five Star and Stadium Club Basketball, Gilded Collection Baseball, Black Diamond Hockey and National Treasures soccer all land before the turkey does. Here's the quick rundown.

Thanksgiving week is stacked. Five new products hit the hobby before Thursday, from high-end hits to photo-driven base sets. Here's what's coming and why collectors care.

Tuesday, Nov 24

  • 2025-26 Topps Five Star Basketball. Five Star is a high-end product built around big autographs and relics. Few cards per box, and every box is about the hits.

Wednesday, Nov 25

  • 2025-26 Topps Stadium Club Basketball. Stadium Club is the set collectors buy for the photography. Full-bleed shots, cool angles, and parallels that look great in a binder.
  • 2026 Topps Gilded Collection Baseball. A premium baseball release with a gold theme, aimed at collectors chasing autographs and numbered cards.
  • 2026-27 Upper Deck Black Diamond Hockey. A hockey favorite known for its diamond-themed design and high-end hits.
  • 2025-26 Panini National Treasures Road to World Cup Soccer. National Treasures is one of the most premium names in the hobby, and the World Cup buzz makes soccer cards a hot chase right now.

Before you buy

Release dates move a lot, especially around holidays. These dates come from the Topps.com release calendar and the Cardsmiths Breaks release calendar, so check with the manufacturer or your shop before you plan around them.
