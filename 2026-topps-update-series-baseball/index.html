2026 Topps Update Series Baseball breaks

The final chapter of the 2026 Topps flagship baseball set: late-season rookies, traded players and short prints, wrapped in Topps' 75th anniversary celebration. What's in a 2026 Topps Update Series Hobby Box, and what we pulled from it live on Whatnot.

What's in a hobby box

  • Hobby Box: 20 packs of 12 cards
  • 1 autograph or relic card per hobby box on average
  • 350-card base set with the Topps 75th Anniversary logo on every base card

About the product

Update Series rounds out the 2026 Topps flagship story with late-season rookies, traded players and the year's big moments. Hobby boxes carry exclusive Rainbow foil parallels (Aqua, Purple /250, Blue /150, Green /99, Gold /50, Orange /25, Black /10, Red /5 and the Foilfractor 1/1).

Inserts include Stat Stars, All Aces, All Kings, Heavy Lumber, Home Field, Autumn Ascensions, Cover Athletes and 1991 Topps Baseball, plus relics like Rising Rookie Relics and City Connect Swatch cards.
