Sell your cards

Got a box of cards in the closet, a stack of slabs, or a whole collection you're done with? Send us photos and we'll come back with a cash or trade offer. No obligation, no pressure.

  1. Send photos

    Fill out the form below and add up to 10 photos of what you've got.

  2. Get an offer

    We look over every submission and email you an offer, or questions if we need a closer look.

  3. Your call

    Take cash, take a trade, or pass. If you say yes, we'll work out the details with you.

What we buy

  • Rookies, autos, relics & numbered parallels
  • Graded slabs (PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC)
  • Sealed hobby boxes, blasters & packs
  • Whole collections, binders & bulk
  • Football, baseball, basketball, hockey & more
  • Older and vintage cards

Questions

How do you come up with an offer?

We look at what the same cards have actually sold for recently, then factor in condition, grade and how many you have. Good photos get you a more accurate offer.

Do I have to accept?

No. Every offer is free and you can say no. If you want to think about it, take your time.

Cash or trade?

Your choice. Pick what you'd like in the form, or choose "cash or trade" and we'll give you options.

Can I just email you?

Sure, email info@on-cards.com with photos and what you've got. The form just makes sure we get everything we need the first time.
