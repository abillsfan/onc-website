2026 Topps Chrome Football breaks

The 2026 NFL Draft class on Chrome Rookie Cards, plus today's stars and a deep insert lineup. What's in 2026 Topps Chrome Football, and what we pulled from it live on Whatnot.

What's in a hobby box

  • Hobby released November 18, 2026
  • 2026 NFL Draft class on Chrome Rookie Cards, alongside current stars and legends
  • Rookie, veteran and legend autographs
  • Numbered Refractor parallels and a long list of inserts

About the product

Topps Chrome Football puts the new rookie class on Chrome. Inserts include Kaiju, Game Genies, Super Bowl at Night, Glass Canvas, Helix, Ultra Violet and Radiating Rookies, with rare Lightning Leaders and Vibes. Topps.com exclusives add their own parallels: X-Fractors in the Mega Box (6 packs of 7 cards), Red White & Blue Refractors in the Value Box (7 packs of 4 cards) and Pulsar parallels in the Hanger Box (one 14-card pack).

The rookie class includes Fernando Mendoza, Jeremiyah Love, Carnell Tate, Jordyn Tyson and Caleb Downs.
