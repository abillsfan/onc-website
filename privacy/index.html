Privacy policy

The short version: we only use your info to send the alerts you asked for and to answer you about your cards. We never sell it.

Effective November 23, 2026. This policy covers On-Cards.com, run by Outta Nowhere Cards ("ONC", "we").

What we collect

  • Show alerts: your email, your first name if you give it, and what you collect.
  • Sell your cards: your name, email, phone if you give it, city or ZIP, details about your cards, and any photos you upload.
  • Basic technical info: the date and time of a form submission and your IP address, to stop spam and abuse.

How we use it

  • To email you when we schedule a show and when we go live, which is what you signed up for.
  • To reply to you about cards you want to sell.
  • That's it. We don't sell, rent or trade your info to anyone.

Email alerts

Every alert email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also reply "unsubscribe" or email info@on-cards.com and we'll take you off the list.

Photos you send us

Photos are only used to look at your cards and make you an offer. Most photos are re-saved when they're uploaded, which removes hidden location data. Ask us any time and we'll delete them.

Buying on Whatnot and eBay

Purchases from our shows and listings happen on Whatnot or eBay. Their own privacy policies cover what you share with them.

Site analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies or track you across other websites.

Your choices

Want to see, change or delete what we have on you? Email info@on-cards.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
