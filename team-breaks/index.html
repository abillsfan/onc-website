Team breaks on Whatnot

Pick your team, watch the boxes get ripped live, and get every card for your team. Here's how team breaks work at Outta Nowhere Cards.

How it works

  1. Buy a team. Each team in the break is sold as its own spot during the live show on Whatnot.
  2. We rip the product live. Every pack is opened on camera, start to finish.
  3. You get every card for your team. Base, rookies, inserts, parallels and hits. All cards ship, base included.

One team per spot

ONC sells teams one at a time. You get your team, not a pairing with a weaker team, unless you specifically ask for a combo. It keeps it simple and fair: you know exactly what you're buying.

Why collectors like team breaks

  • Chase your favorite team or player collection (PC) in high-end product
  • Pay for one team instead of a whole box
  • Every card for your team, not just the hits
  • Live on camera, so you see every pull

What we break

Any card, any sport, and the product changes show to show. See every product we've broken in What we break, what's coming out in the release calendar, and what's next on On Deck.

Questions

How much is a team?

Team prices depend on the product and the team, and they're set during the show on Whatnot. Follow outta_nowhere_cards to see the lineup before it goes live.

When does my break run?

During a scheduled show on Whatnot. Follow outta_nowhere_cards and turn on alerts to catch it. Full details in the break rules.

What if my team doesn't hit?

Every card for your team is yours, hit or not. That's part of breaking: some teams hit, some don't, and every pack is opened on camera so you see it all.

Can I buy two teams together?

Yes, just ask in the chat. Teams are sold one at a time by default, and combos are available when you request one.

Grab your team at the next break

Follow outta_nowhere_cards and turn on alerts so you know the second we go live. Show announcements and hits land on Facebook first.
