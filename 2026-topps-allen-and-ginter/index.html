2026 Topps Allen & Ginter breaks

Minis, celebrity autographs, rip cards and a deep rookie class. Here's what's in 2026 Topps Allen & Ginter, and what we've pulled from it live on Whatnot.

What's in a hobby box

  • Released October 7, 2026
  • 24 packs per box, 8 cards per pack, 12 boxes per case
  • 2 hits per hobby box: autograph, relic, rip card or printing plate
  • 300-card base set, plus minis, inserts and parallels

What makes A&G different

Allen & Ginter mixes baseball with celebrities and non-sports subjects. The checklist includes framed mini autographs, full-size autographs, relics, and rip cards: hobby-exclusive cards you can keep sealed or tear open for an exclusive mini inside. Parallels include Foil Filigree (Blue /150, Green /99, Gold /50, Orange /25, Black /10, Red /5 and 1/1s), along with Chrome, mini metal, stained glass and wood minis.

The rookie class includes Munetaka Murakami, Kyle Teel, Bryce Eldridge, Carson Benge, Justin Crawford, Owen Caissie and Trey Yesavage.
