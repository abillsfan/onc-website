2026 Topps Holiday Football breaks

Holiday-themed football cards: this year's NFL rookie class and veteran stars in festive designs, glitter and metallic parallels, and a full Chrome version of the base set. What's in a 2026 Topps Holiday Football Mega Box, and what we pulled from it live on Whatnot.

What's in a hobby box

  • Released October 23, 2026
  • Mega Box: 10 packs of 10 cards (100 cards)
  • Full Chrome version of the base set, with Refractors, Sparkles and numbered parallels to the Superfractor 1/1

About the product

Topps Holiday comes to the gridiron with glitter and metallic holiday parallels, holiday short-print variations, Dear Santa Autographed Relics, autographs, Holiday Spirit Variations, and inserts like Hidden Elf, Snow Globe Moments, Deck the Fields and Frozen Fields.

The rookie class includes Fernando Mendoza, Ty Simpson, Jeremiyah Love, Carnell Tate, Makai Lemon, Jordyn Tyson, Arvell Reese and Caleb Downs.
